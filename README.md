# GTC Product Identity Catalog

## Overview

GTC Product Identity Catalog is a decentralized infrastructure layer for intelligent global trade.

It introduces **Product Identity Objects** — canonical, consumer-centric representations of products that exist independently of marketplaces, sellers, or brands.

These objects are:
- created and maintained by AI,
- secured and monetized via blockchain,
- governed through DAO mechanisms.

---

## Problem

Global trade operates on fragmented product data:
- duplicated SKUs,
- inconsistent descriptions,
- marketing-driven listings,
- no universal product identity.

This prevents AI agents from making optimal decisions and creates inefficiency for buyers, sellers, and producers.

---

## Solution

GTC creates a new abstraction layer:

**Product Identity**
- describes what a product *does* and *who it is for*,
- aggregates all offers, sellers, logistics, and prices,
- evolves through AI learning and validation.

---

## Architecture

### 1. AI & Data Layer
- Marketplace data ingestion
- Feature extraction
- Consumer Need Profiling
- Deduplication and clustering

### 2. Product Identity Catalog
- Canonical product objects
- Versioning and validation
- Platform-neutral references

### 3. Blockchain & DAO Layer
- Product Identity Tokens (NFT)
- Revenue sharing
- Governance and arbitration

---

## Tokenization Model

Each Product Identity can be tokenized as an NFT:
- ownership represents rights to revenue participation,
- tokens can be auctioned or fractionally owned,
- commissions are distributed automatically via smart contracts.

---

## Economic Model

Revenue is generated from:
- transaction-based commissions,
- primary auctions of Product Identity Tokens,
- secondary market fees.

Incentives are aligned across:
- buyers,
- sellers,
- producers,
- platform,
- DAO.

---

## Governance

DAO governance ensures:
- data integrity,
- fair economic rules,
- dispute resolution,
- long-term evolution of the catalog.

AI proposes changes, DAO approves them.
